QUICK ANSWERS

Before you enquire.

These answers help you send the right information the first time.

What should I send when asking for a used tyre?

Send the tyre size printed on the sidewall, for example 205/55 R16, plus your vehicle model if you know it.

Can every damaged tyre be repaired?

No. Repair suitability depends on the damage, its location and the tyre's condition. Safety comes first.

How do I contact TyreBoss?

Email tyrebossmft@gmail.com with your tyre size, vehicle details and what you need.
